1. Financial inventory: moving-average valuation and cost of goods sold, or defer it for a non-stock first release?

3. Tax posting: model tax liabilities and credits according to verified Iranian rules.
4. Fiscal-year closing: locking, adjustments, reopening authority and reporting.
5. AI autonomy: approval policy, transaction boundaries and multi-step error recovery.
6. Production acceptance: browser UX, RTL/Jalali inputs, accessibility, backups and security review.
